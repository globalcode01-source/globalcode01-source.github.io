Son güncelleme: 9 Kasım 2026 · KVKK

AllFixDoc bir PDF okuyucu ve düzenleyicidir. Bu politika, uygulamanın hangi verileri neden işlediğini açıklar.

1. Belgelerin

AllFixDoc yalnızca senin seçtiğin dosyaları açar (Android dosya seçicisi ya da "Birlikte aç" ile). Depolama izni istemez, cihazını taramaz. Belgeler cihazında işlenir ve uygulama tarafından yüklenmez; tek istisna, AI asistanını kullandığında 4. maddede anlatılan durumdur.

2. Cihazında saklanan veriler

Uygulama şunları saklar: son açılan dosyaların listesi ve küçük önizleme görselleri, yer imleri ve okuma konumları, OCR ile tanınan metinler, çizdiğin imzalar, uygulama ayarları ve eklediysen seçtiğin AI servisinin API anahtarı (cihazının güvenli donanımındaki bir anahtarla şifrelenmiş olarak). Bu verileri Ayarlar'dan ya da uygulamanın depolama alanını temizleyerek silebilirsin. Sunum modunda yazdığın konuşmacı notlarını ve açtığın sunumlardan çıkarılan video ile sesleri de saklar; bunlar yedeklenmez. Sertifikalı imzada sertifika dosyası ve parolası yalnızca imza sırasında cihazında kullanılır; uygulama bunları kopyalamaz, saklamaz ve göndermez.

3. Yedekleme

Android yedeklemesi açıksa Android; ayarlarını, yer imlerini ve okuma konumlarını Google hesabına yedekleyebilir. API anahtarın, imzaların, OCR sonuçları, önizleme görselleri ve son açılanlar listesi yedeklemeye dahil edilmez. İstersen yedek dosyasını kendin de dışa ve içe aktarabilirsin.

4. AI asistanı (isteğe bağlı)

AI asistanı yalnızca seçtiğin servis için bir API anahtarı eklersen çalışır: Google Gemini (varsayılan), Anthropic Claude, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter ya da kendin eklediğin başka bir servis. Servis bu verileri kendi koşulları ve gizlilik politikası (ör. policies.google.com, anthropic.com/legal) kapsamında işler. Anahtarlar cihazında şifreli saklanır. AllFixDoc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AllFixDoc is a PDF reader and editor. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Your documents

AllFixDoc opens only the files you choose, through the Android file picker or "Open with". It doesn't request storage permission and doesn't scan your device. Documents are processed on your device and aren't uploaded by the app, except as described in section 4 when you use the AI assistant.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the list of recently opened files and small preview images, bookmarks and reading positions, text recognized with OCR, signatures you draw, app settings and, if you add one, the API key of the AI service you chose, encrypted with a key held in your device's secure hardware. You can remove this data in Settings or by clearing the app's storage. In presentation mode it also keeps the speaker notes you write and the videos and sounds taken from presentations you open; these are not backed up. For a certificate signature, the certificate file and its password are used on your device only while signing; the app doesn't copy, keep or send them.

3. Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may back up your settings, bookmarks and reading positions to your Google account. Your API key, signatures, OCR results, preview images and the recent files list are excluded. You can also export and import a backup file yourself.

4. AI assistant (optional)

The AI assistant works only after you add an API key for the service you choose: Google Gemini (default), Anthropic Claude, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter or another service you add yourself. When you ask a question or request a summary, the text of the pages you selected and your messages are sent over an encrypted connection to that service only, using your key. The service processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy (for example policies.google.com, anthropic.com/legal). Keys are stored encrypted on your device. The developer of AllFixDoc doesn't receive this data. Dictating notes uses your device's speech recognition service (for example Google); AllFixDoc doesn't record or keep audio.

5. Text recognition, code reading and scanning

OCR, QR and barcode reading and document scanning use Google ML Kit through Google Play services. Images are processed on your device. Google Play services may collect diagnostic and usage information about these features under Google's privacy policy. If your phone's language isn't built into the app, the automatic translation downloads a Google ML Kit translation model once (over Wi‑Fi) and translates the interface on your device; no text leaves the device.

6. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads at the bottom of the Home, Files and Tools screens through Google AdMob, and occasionally a full-screen ad after a tool finishes its work. No ads are shown while you read or edit documents. In the European Economic Area and the UK you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If you want to use a Pro tool once without buying, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad; it is only shown when you tap it.

7. Purchases

If you buy the ad-free Pro version, payment is handled by Google Play. The app only receives whether the purchase is active. The yearly subscription renews automatically through Google Play until you cancel it in Google Play › Subscriptions.

Remote settings file

When the app starts it downloads a small settings file (ads and purchases) from the developer's site (globalcode01-source.github.io, hosted on GitHub). Nothing but your IP address is sent with this request.

GDPR and KVKK

Data controller: Global Code 01 (globalcode01@gmail.com). The developer does not collect your documents or personal data; only if you write to us are your e-mail address and message used to reply.

Transfers abroad: Google (AdMob, ML Kit, Play), GitHub and the AI service you choose (Google, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, etc.) run servers outside your country. If you decline, nothing is sent and only that feature stays off; the startup settings file and ads are not loaded without consent either. You can give or withdraw consent any time in Settings › Privacy › Transfers abroad.
